Failsafe-

Simulates a dead transmitter: no pilot packets at all. After "Link t/o" (2 s) the flight controller starts its failsafe. Return home = climbs, flies home, hovers at 2 m until you move a stick or someone holds it. With no GPS or compass it lands where it is.

Good to know

      
Position hold ON (default): let go of the sticks and the drone brakes and stays on its spot, even in
        wind. SPEED sticks: the stick sets the speed (NORMAL: full stick = 5 m/s). ANGLE sticks: the stick sets the tilt.

      
Position hold OFF: it holds height and heading only and keeps drifting like a DJI drone in ATTI mode
        (it has almost no air drag): steer the other way to stop it.

      
Gamepad + keyboard: both work together; a pressed key overrides the gamepad. Gamepad sticks have a 25 %
        deadzone, because thumbsticks often rest off-centre after use. A red warning appears in card 3 if one still does.

      
RC link test: "Cut RC link" stops all pilot packets. After 2 s the failsafe lands it on the spot.

      
Press a key once on the page if the keyboard seems dead (the page needs focus).
        Gamepads appear after you press a button on them.
