Good to know

      
The drone holds its height and heading when you let go. It has almost no air drag, so it keeps drifting:
        steer the other way to stop it, like a DJI drone in ATTI mode.

      
Press a key once on the page if the keyboard seems dead (the page needs focus).
        Gamepads appear after you press a button on them.
